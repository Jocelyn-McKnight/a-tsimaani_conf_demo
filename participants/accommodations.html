Accommodation

We have secured group room blocks at several hotels near the conference. These hotels offer preferred conference rates and convenient access to venues and local amenities. Availability is limited, so we recommend booking early.

Coast Lethbridge Hotel & Conference Centre

This is the primary conference hotel. If you are staying here, you will not need transportation on Days 2–4. Please note that Day 1 will take place at the University of Lethbridge Campus.
