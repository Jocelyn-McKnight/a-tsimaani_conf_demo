Parking and Directions

Day 1 (March 9) will take place on campus at Iniskim University of Lethbridge in the University Hall Atrium. Parking passes are available for registered attendees. If you need a parking pass, please email atsimaaniconference@uleth.ca and we will send you a printable pass. Additional passes can also be picked up from the registration desk at the conference. Designated parking will be in Lot F only, and signs will guide you to the conference location from there. For parking locations, see the University of Lethbridge campus parking maps .

Days 2–4 (March 10–12) will take place at the Coast Hotel and Conference Centre, 526 Mayor Magrath Drive South in Lethbridge, where parking is free.
