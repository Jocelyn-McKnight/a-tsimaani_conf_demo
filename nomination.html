Nominate a Niitsi’powahsini Language Champion!

Do you know someone whose language work deserves to be recognized?

A Niitsi'powahsini Language Champion is someone who has put work towards the revitalization, rematriation, teaching, maintenance and support of the Blackfoot language. We want to recognize their work at a ceremony during the conference banquet on Monday March 9, 2026.

Please send an email to atsimaaniconference@uleth.ca by February 18th, 2026 with the following information:

  • Name of nominee(s)
  • Their role or function
  • Their tribal affiliation
  • Reason(s) why they should be recognized at the ceremony
